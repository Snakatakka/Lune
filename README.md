# GeodeMusicPlayer

The GeodeMusicPlayer is/was my (very poor) attempt at making a desktop app that emulates the *feel* of an mp3 player like those from the 2000's-2010's. Over time it became less and less about making something that felt like an mp3 player from the 2000's, and just making an mp3 player that felt good to use.

The GeodeMusicPlayer can do anything pretty much anything that any respectable music player should do
